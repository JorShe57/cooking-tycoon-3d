How to Play

  1. Customers line up at the pass (back counter) with an order.
  2. Tap a crate to grab an ingredient. You can carry one thing at a time.
  3. Tap a cutting board to drop lettuce/tomato, then tap again to chop 🔪.
  4. Put bread, patties or diced tomato on a burner 🔥. Grab it when it dings — before it burns!
  5. Tap a plate to add food. Tap it with empty hands to pick the plate up.
  6. Tap the pass to serve. Faster service = bigger tips.
  7. Mistakes go in the trash 🗑️ (−$2).
  8. Between days, spend cash on upgrades. Finish 10 career days!
