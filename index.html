How to Play

  1. Plan each day: choose the menu and prices, buy ingredients, pick the day's event, hire staff.
  2. Customers line up at the pass (back counter). Their tickets show what to make.
  3. Tap stations to send your chef there — taps queue up, so plan your route!
  4. Crates give ingredients (uses stock; if empty you pay 3× for a rush delivery).
  5. Boards 🔪 chop, burners 🔥 cook, fryers 🛢️ fry. Grab food before it burns!
  6. Add food to a plate, pick the plate up, tap the pass to serve. Faster = bigger tips.
  7. From day 3 plates come back dirty — scrub them at the sink 🧽.
  8. Use 🛠 Kitchen to buy and rearrange stations. Short walks = faster service.
  9. Grow your rank, finish milestones and weekly challenges, and open a Food Truck!
